# SpendVoice privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-25

SpendVoice is a personal expense tracker. It is designed so that your financial information never leaves your device.

## What we store

Each expense you save (amount, currency, category, date, and the text you typed or spoke) is stored in your browser's local database on your device. We do not run servers that receive this data.

## What leaves your device

- **We send nothing.** SpendVoice has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.
- **Voice input is handled by your browser.** When you press Speak, your browser starts speech recognition. Some browsers, including Chrome and Edge, may send the audio to their speech service to convert it to text. That service is operated by the browser maker, not by SpendVoice. Check your browser's privacy settings if this matters to you.

## Your controls

- Delete any expense in the app.
- Export all expenses as a CSV file.
- Clear your site data in your browser settings to remove everything.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too, and the change is recorded in the project history.
